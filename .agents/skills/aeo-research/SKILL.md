# AEO Research and Strategy

## Research

- Collect real questions from Search Console, customer support, sales, site search, People Also Ask, autocomplete and reliable keyword/question tools where available.
- Group questions by intent and funnel stage.
- Identify pages that should answer each question.
- Review whether the answer is direct, accurate, complete, current and supported.
- Identify eligible answer formats: concise definition, steps, comparison, table, troubleshooting, FAQ or calculation.
- Check whether the page's answer is visible and accessible in rendered HTML.
- Evaluate structured data only where supported and appropriate.

## Content format

For each target question:
- Use a natural question as a heading when useful.
- Answer directly near the beginning.
- Add context, steps, caveats and evidence.
- Use lists/tables only when they genuinely clarify.
- Attribute expert or first-hand claims.
- Avoid filler and repetitive FAQ blocks.
- Keep answers aligned with the actual product/service and user intent.

## Measurement

Record featured snippet/answer result observations with exact query, locale, device, timestamp, source and URL. Distinguish observed appearance from eligibility. Structured data does not guarantee a featured snippet or answer placement.

## Output

Produce `aeo-strategy.md` with question clusters, target URLs, proposed answer content, format, evidence, priority and validation.
