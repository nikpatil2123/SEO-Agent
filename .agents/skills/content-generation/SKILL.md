# Content Generation

## Inputs

Use the approved business brief, audit, competitor research, SEO/GEO/AEO strategies, brand voice, legal constraints and verified product facts.

## Deliverables

For each priority URL, prepare:
- Proposed SEO title and meta description.
- Search intent and primary topic.
- Proposed H1 and heading outline.
- Full replacement/additional visible copy when needed.
- Product/service facts and differentiators supported by evidence.
- Useful question-and-answer content.
- Internal link recommendations.
- Image alt text recommendations (do not change decorative image alt semantics).
- Structured data proposal with verified values only.
- CTA recommendation that preserves existing conversion behavior.
- Source/evidence list and unresolved fact checks.

## Quality controls

- Original writing; do not copy competitor passages.
- No fabricated reviews, statistics, case studies, certifications, guarantees, authors or product specifications.
- No unsupported superlatives or comparative claims.
- No keyword stuffing or repetitive AI-generated filler.
- Match the customer's audience, terminology and brand voice.
- Cite authoritative sources for factual claims where appropriate.
- Flag medical, financial, legal, safety or regulated claims for qualified review.
- Identify every visible text addition/rewrite as **CONSENT REQUIRED**.
- Do not publish or edit the live site.

## Structured data

Generate JSON-LD only when the page content supports the chosen schema type. Values must match visible, verified page content. Do not add fake aggregate ratings, reviews, prices, availability, authorship or business details. Avoid duplicate/conflicting schema graphs.

## Output

Produce `content-proposals.md` with exact proposed text, affected URLs, rationale, evidence, and consent status. Keep proposal content separate from live files until approved.
