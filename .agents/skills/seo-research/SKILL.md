# SEO Research and Strategy

## Research

- Map seed terms to topic clusters and relevant landing pages.
- Classify intent: informational, navigational, commercial investigation, transactional, local, or mixed.
- Record keyword source, date, geography, language, volume/difficulty only when sourced, and whether metrics are estimates.
- Map one primary intent/topic to an appropriate page; avoid creating near-duplicate pages to target keyword variants.
- Identify content gaps against the researched top-five results.
- Review internal link paths, crawlability, indexability, canonicalization and page experience.
- Assess local SEO only where the business serves a defined local area.
- Assess product SEO where applicable: product identifiers, availability, price accuracy, merchant feeds and eligible structured data.

## Strategy requirements

For every target page specify:
- Primary topic/query and intent
- Secondary entities and related questions
- Recommended title/meta description
- Heading/content outline
- Internal link targets and anchor rationale
- Structured data type, if appropriate
- Technical remediation
- Conversion intent and CTA constraints
- Evidence, priority, effort, risk and validation

## Rules

- Search volume is not search intent.
- Do not keyword-stuff or create doorway pages.
- Do not create pages solely to capture near-identical queries.
- Do not promise ranking positions.
- Do not claim a ranking or traffic change without a dated measurement source.
- Visible copy changes require separate end-user consent.

## Output

Produce `seo-strategy.md` with prioritized, page-specific recommendations and measurable baseline metrics.
