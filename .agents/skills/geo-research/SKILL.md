# GEO Research and Strategy

## Objective

Improve the likelihood that generative search systems can understand the business and may select reliable pages as sources. Do not guarantee AI mentions or citations.

## Research dimensions

1. Entity clarity: consistent brand, organization, product, people and location facts.
2. Source quality: original information, transparent authorship, evidence, references and first-hand expertise.
3. Factual completeness: clear product/service details, specifications, use cases, limitations and policies.
4. Brand consistency: consistent name, contact information, descriptions and official profiles across trusted sources.
5. Topic coverage: useful pages that answer real customer needs, with clear relationships between concepts.
6. Citation readiness: claims are attributable, specific, current and easy to verify.
7. Technical accessibility: crawlable, renderable, stable canonical URLs and useful page structure.
8. AI visibility tests: run a documented set of representative prompts on available platforms and record whether the brand/page is mentioned or cited.

## Measurement protocol

For each platform/prompt record:
- Platform and interface/API
- Date/time
- User location/locale and language, where known
- Exact prompt
- Response capture/reference
- Brand mention (yes/no)
- Exact URL citation (yes/no)
- Citation position/context
- Competitors mentioned/cited
- Whether result is repeatable (repeat tests where feasible)
- Limitations and access method

Do not infer AI visibility from schema presence, crawler access, or generic content quality. Do not claim an AI system uses a specific file or markup unless supported by documentation or evidence.

## Content recommendations

Prioritize original research, first-hand expertise, transparent sourcing, specific factual answers, clear entity references, and maintained pages. Avoid synthetic "AI bait", fabricated statistics, mass-produced low-value pages and unsupported claims.

## Output

Produce `geo-strategy.md` with observed visibility, entity/content gaps, platform-specific opportunities, evidence and confidence.
