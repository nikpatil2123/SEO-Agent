# Business and Product Discovery

## Objective

Build a verified business context before keyword selection or content recommendations.

## Required fields

- Business/brand name and canonical website.
- Products/services and product taxonomy.
- Primary and secondary value propositions.
- Differentiators/USPs, with customer evidence.
- Target audience, buyer roles and customer problems.
- Target countries, regions, languages and service areas.
- Business model (B2B, B2C, marketplace, SaaS, local service, publisher, etc.).
- Purchase/lead/conversion journey and primary CTA.
- Priority products, services, categories and URLs.
- Known competitors and alternatives.
- Regulatory, legal, claims, brand voice and content constraints.
- Existing proof: certifications, case studies, reviews, authors, warranties, specifications.
- Excluded products, locations, audiences or topics.

## Process

1. Inspect homepage, about, product/service, contact, policy and priority pages.
2. Extract only claims actually supported by the site or customer.
3. Ask targeted follow-up questions for material gaps.
4. Separate facts into:
   - Customer-confirmed
   - Website-observed
   - Third-party verified
   - Hypothesis / unverified
5. Resolve contradictions with the customer; do not silently choose one.
6. Produce `business-understanding.md`.

## Do not

- Infer product claims from a brand name alone.
- Invent target demographics, market share, certifications, guarantees or testimonials.
- Assume the target country from hosting location.
- assume every visitor is a buyer.
- Start keyword targeting before product and search intent are clear.

## Completion criteria

The business brief identifies the offer, audience, geography, conversion goal, priority URLs and known constraints, or explicitly lists unresolved questions.
