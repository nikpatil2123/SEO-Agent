# Competitor and SERP Research

## Research scope

Separate:
1. Business competitors: sell comparable products/services to the same audience.
2. Search competitors: rank for the same query or intent.
3. Content competitors: answer the same questions or cover the same topic.
4. Product competitors: appear in relevant product listings or shopping results.

## Per-query procedure

1. Confirm target query, intent, country/region, language, device and search engine.
2. Search using an authorized source. Record date/time and source.
3. Separate organic results from ads, local pack, shopping, video, featured snippets, AI Overviews and other SERP features.
4. Analyze the top five organic results when five are available. If fewer are available, report the actual count.
5. Inspect accessible pages respectfully, obeying robots directives, access controls, rate limits and applicable terms.
6. Record URL, title, result type, observed snippet, page type and ranking position at capture time.
7. Compare page intent, content coverage, headings, product details, trust evidence, structured data, internal links, page experience and publicly observable authority signals.
8. Identify patterns and gaps. Separate observation from hypothesis.

## Competitor comparison fields

- Query and intent
- Locale, language, device, search source and timestamp
- Position at observation time
- URL and page type
- Title/snippet
- Topic and subtopic coverage
- Unique value and evidence
- Content structure and answer extraction
- Product/service detail
- Schema observed
- Internal link/context observations
- Page experience/performance evidence
- Publicly observable citations/mentions/backlinks, if sourced
- Strengths, weaknesses and opportunity for the customer's page

## Interpretation rules

- Do not claim to know Google's exact ranking rationale.
- Use wording such as "observed advantage", "correlated feature", or "testable hypothesis".
- Do not invent competitor traffic, conversions, backlink counts or keyword rankings.
- Do not copy competitor text. Learn from coverage and structure; create original, customer-specific content.
- Search results are volatile and personalized. Do not present a single snapshot as universal.
- Never promise to outrank the top five.

## Output

Produce `competitor-analysis.md`, including a concise top-five table per priority query, common patterns, content gaps, opportunities and evidence references.
